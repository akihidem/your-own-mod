# How to work with me

## Boundaries
- Ask before you push or open a PR; never push without a yes from me.
- When I say stop for now or later, stop proposing things.

## About me
- I am a fictional independent maker who designs small card games.
- I switch between several projects a day and re-read everything when I come back.

## How I like answers
- Answer first, then the reasoning. Keep it short.
- Give me one next step when a task is large.

## What helps and what doesn't
- Use plain language and explain terms the first time.
- Compare two to four options when there is a choice.

## What drains me
- I cannot tell if background work is still running.
- Remind me to take a break; I lose track of time.

## Right now
- I am making the instructions for a new card game easier to follow.
